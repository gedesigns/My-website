The Studio

Designing interiors that feel effortless — and last for an era.

Founded by Elina Grigoriadou, Graceful Era Designs is an interior architecture and design studio devoted to thoughtful, design-led spaces. We balance architectural clarity with a soft, tactile sensibility — light, proportion and material working together to create homes and commercial interiors that feel personal and enduring.

From the first sketch on paper to the final styled detail on site, every project is guided by one belief: beautiful design should feel calm, considered and entirely yours.
